### 2. The Real Price Breakdown (Where the Money Goes)

A standard Copa Airlines Economy Basic ticket on `LAS ⇄ ASU` (~$750) breaks down like this:

* **Base Airfare:** ~$460 – $520
* **US Government Taxes & Passenger Facility Charges:** ~$85
* **Paraguayan International Arrival & Airport Taxes:** ~$78
* **Panama Transit Facility Fees:** ~$20
* **Panama Stopover Tax (if leaving Tocumen Airport for 24h+):** ~$40 – $50
* **Total:** **~$683 – $793**

> **Note on Baggage:** The lowest fares on Copa ("Basic") include **1 personal item + 1 carry-on** (up to 22 lbs). If you need a checked bag (up to 50 lbs) for a 4–6 week trip, Copa charges **\$40 to \$50 each way** at online check-in.

### 3. How to Execute the Panama Stopover (Step-by-Step)

If you take advantage of the 20+ hour stopover in Panama City, do **not** book two separate one-way tickets (which would cost \$1,200+). Use Copa's Stopover engine:

1. Open the [Copa Airlines Panama Stopover Portal](https://www.copaair.com/en-us/panama-stopover/).
2. Select **"Panama Stopover"** (or select **"Multi-City"**).
3. Input:
   * **Segment 1:** `LAS` to `PTY` on **Tue, May 5** (arrives ~9:30 AM).
   * **Segment 2:** `PTY` to `ASU` on **Wed, May 6** (departs ~3:30 PM, giving you **30 hours** in Panama).
   * **Segment 3:** `ASU` to `LAS` on **Wed, Jun 9** (standard ~3h transit in PTY on the way back).
4. The system calculates the base round-trip fare without double airfare penalties.
